# 06 — Proposals for v6 (after the broad test)

> Status: **implemented in prototype 0.5** (2026-11-23). Results: [simulations/broad-test.md](simulations/broad-test.md)
> (comparison with v0.4 at the end). Proposal based on [simulations/broad-test-v0.4-baseline.md](simulations/broad-test-v0.4-baseline.md).
> Goal, in the user's words: *some consistency, and it can be moulded, but not too repetitive, with
> frequent surprises from the creature. Full sessions 5–10 min when fully developed. A new stage
> about every 3 sessions. It keeps adapting to new tendencies even when fully developed. The initial
> character and early experiences persist somewhat, but are eventually replaced or modified.*
>
> Each proposal names the problem it fixes and a **measurable target** for the next broad test.

## Overview

| # | Change | Fixes | Target in the broad test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Growth by **experience**, not only by rules | no growth without strict teaching; too fast, then stuck | stages at ~3 / 6 / 9 / 12 sessions for engaged players; ≤ 5 sessions per stage for loose players |
| 2 | **Self-boredom**: it notices its own loops | loops, never bored | repeated 3-emoji sequences < 15%; "uneasy" dominant in some sessions |
| 3 | **Habit fatigue** within a session | loops | habits still recognisable across sessions, varied within one |
| 4 | **Meaningful surprises** instead of babble | surprises are mostly noise | ≥ 1 noticeable surprise per minute; 2–3 new emojis per session |
| 5 | **Recency-weighted "frequently used"** | early emojis dominate the keyboard forever | shifter: new language > 40% within ~6 sessions after the switch |
| 6 | **Unlearning**: habits that are challenged fade faster | old habits stick | old answers < 20% after ~4 sessions |
| 7 | **Living character** | fixed for life; early experiences lock in | inborn emojis still visible after a week, but half replaced after ~2 weeks of other play; early-day emojis < 50% of its moves by day 10 for players who change |
| 8 | **Wider emotional range** | only joyful / curious | each emotion strongest in at least some sessions for some player types |
| 9 | **Session length tweak** | stage 4 ≈ 9.8 min (upper end) | stage 4 ≈ 6–8 min |

---

## 1. Growth by experience

**Now:** the stage only depends on complexity, i.e. how many confident rules exist. Strict teachers
create many rules at once (stage 3 by session 5), then the language consolidates and complexity
stalls. Players who don't teach strictly never create rules, so the creature stays a newborn forever.

**Proposal:** growth comes from **maturity points** collected per session. Every session adds
points, and *how* you play changes the speed:
- **engagement:** up to 1 point for a full session (scaled by inputs, capped)
- **learning bonus:** +up to 0.5 for new rules/habits formed, inventions picked up, questions answered
- **one stage ≈ 3 points**, so ~3 engaged sessions per stage. Rich play gets there a bit faster
  (~2), sparse play slower (~4–5).
- **Shrinking** stays possible, but only from long neglect (days without play), not from a
  consolidated language.
- Complexity stays as a measure of what it knows, and later drives appearance details. It no
  longer gates growth.

## 2. Self-boredom

**Now:** boredom only rises when *your* moves are predictable. The random keyboard half keeps you
surprising, so it never gets bored, even when it says the same things over and over.

**Proposal:** a second boredom source: **its own repetitiveness**. If the last ~20 emojis contain
many repeated 3-emoji sequences (loops), unease (bored) rises. When bored it:
- breaks out of the loop: twist, new sign, invented game, or an old memory (déjà vu)
- gets more chaotic for a few turns

## 3. Habit fatigue

**Proposal:** every time a habit or rule plays out in a session, it gets a little **tired** (−15%
pull per use, recovering fully during sleep). Habits stay recognisable from session to session (the
first time you send 🌞 today, it still says 🌻), but within a session it starts varying them: 🌼,
🌻🌻, or something new. Consistency **across** sessions, variety **within** one.

## 4. Meaningful surprises

**Now:** most of its "initiative" is small variations, random babbling and its inborn emojis.

**Proposal:** shift that share toward surprises that feel intentional:
- **memories:** déjà vu brings back an old thread (from stage 2, not only stage 3)
- **combinations:** the start of one narrative continued with the end of another
- **twists:** a similar emoji from the sleep brain (🌻 → 🌼)
- **new signs:** 2–3 per session, from the neighbourhood of what you talk about
- **games:** from stage 2, more often when bored or joyful

Less pure babble after stage 1. Target: roughly one noticeable surprise per minute of play.

## 5. Recency-weighted "frequently used"

**Now:** "frequently used" counts every emoji you ever sent. After a few days the early emojis
fill it forever, so new habits of yours can't get established (the shifter's new language never
took off).

**Proposal:** counts fade over time (half-life ~3 sessions), like the iOS keyboard. What you use now
is what you see; old favourites drift out unless you keep using them. The five signs stay pinned.

## 6. Unlearning

**Now:** a habit only disappears after 3 sleeps without confirmation. When you answer differently,
the old habit keeps pulling.

**Proposal:** when you **contradict** a habit (you answer something else after the same emoji,
or ❌ it), it loses strength immediately. After two contradicted sessions it's no longer a habit.
New tendencies can replace old ones within a few sessions, while untouched habits stay.

## 7. Living character

**Now:** six inborn emojis, fixed forever, equally strong. Early experiences lock in (80–89% of
its emoji moves on day 10 still use emojis from days 1–2).

**Proposal:** the character becomes a **weighted set that evolves during sleep**:
- inborn emojis start with high weight, and their weight **fades slowly** (e.g. −8% per sleep) unless
  they get reinforced
- emojis gain weight when it uses them and joy follows, when you pick them up, or when they're part
  of its habits
- its **character = the 6 strongest**. The nature pull, babbling and the neighbourhood of its
  experiments follow the current character.
- result: the inborn character shapes the first days and persists if you engage with it, and is
  gradually replaced by what it has lived through. A fully developed creature keeps absorbing new
  impulses into its character.
- everything is still derived from the history (the "history is the creature" rule holds). The
  character is also the natural input for its later appearance.

## 8. Wider emotional range

- **Praise wears off:** repeated ✅ / ❤️ within a short time give less and less joy.
- **Curiosity wears off:** many new emojis in a row turn curiosity into confusion (uneasy), not
  more curiosity.
- **Sadness:** stronger when its questions or inventions are ignored, or after several ❌ in a row.
- **Anger:** also when its habits are contradicted repeatedly (from #6), not only when it was very
  sure.
- **Associations relative to its usual mood:** an emoji is "joyful" if joy is *higher than usual*
  when it appears, not just because it appears often.

## 9. Session length

Devoted players: stage 4 ≈ 9.8 min (116 inputs). Lower the stage capacities slightly
(stage 4 ≈ 90 inputs ≈ 7–8 min) so the fully developed creature sits in the middle of 5–10 min.

---

## Suggested order

1. **#1 growth**, **#5 keyboard recency**, **#9 session length**: simple, large effect.
2. **#2 self-boredom**, **#3 habit fatigue**, **#6 unlearning**: the core of "consistent but not
   repetitive, and adaptable".
3. **#7 living character**, **#8 emotional range**, **#4 surprises**: identity and liveliness.

After each step: re-run `sh sim/run-broad.sh` and compare with the targets above.

## As implemented (0.5), tuned with the broad test

- **#1 growth:** maturity per session = min(1, inputs/30) + learning bonus (≤ 0.2). Stages at 3 / 6 / 9
  / 12 points, so stages arrive at sessions ~4 / 7 / 9 / 12. Maturity is capped at 15. After 2 days
  without play it loses 0.5 points per day, and it shrinks a stage below 70% of the threshold.
- **#2 self-boredom:** > 20% repeated 3-emoji sequences in the last 20 emojis → uneasy (bored).
- **#3 habit fatigue:** ×0.75 per use when continuing its own chains, ×0.92 when replying to you. What
  fatigue takes away goes 60% to twists and 40% to new or remembered emojis. Resets in sleep.
- **#4 surprises:**
  - memories: 35% of its "new" impulses from stage 2 bring back an old emoji
  - déjà vu and games from stage 2
  - less babble when older
  - "no idea" moments at higher stages are improvised (twist / new / memory) instead of waiting
- **#5 keyboard:** "frequently used" fades with a half-life of 3 sessions (`src/core/frequent.js`).
- **#6 unlearning:**
  - habits have a strength: −0.1 when you answer differently (mild, because changing the topic
    looks the same), −0.5 on ❌, +0.15 when confirmed
  - a habit below 0.6 at sleep starts over; below 0.3 it's dropped
  - habit pull is scaled by strength
- **#7 living character:**
  - inborn emojis start at weight 2 and fade ×0.97 per sleep
  - others gain at most 0.1 per sleep (from ✅/❤️ on its moves, picked-up inventions, habits, and
    what you used ≥ 3× in a session)
  - character = the 6 strongest
- **#8 emotions:**
  - praise and novelty wear off when repeated
  - unanswered questions and ❌ streaks bring sadness (and anger)
  - curiosity is calmer
  - emoji associations are measured relative to its usual mood
- **#9 session length:** stage capacities 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.65 / 0.8 / 0.95 (stage 4 ≈ 80 inputs ≈ 6.6
  min).
- Test panel: maturity and next stage, character with weights, habit strengths.
