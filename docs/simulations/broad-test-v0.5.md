# Broad test: 7 player types × 3 seeds × 10 days

Generated by `sim/broad.js` · engine 0.5.0 · default parameters · seeds 101, 202, 303 · 30 s.
Values are averages over the 3 seeds.

## Player types

- **teacher**: consistent language, teaches with ✅ ❤️ ❌ ❓, demonstrates when it waits. 3 sessions/day.
- **devoted**: like teacher, but plays every session until it falls asleep. 3 sessions/day.
- **shifter**: like teacher, but from day 6 on switches to a completely new language and new favourite emojis. 3 sessions/day.
- **affectionate**: lots of ❤️, loose language (60% consistent), sometimes picks up its emojis, never ❌. 3 sessions/day.
- **mirror**: mirrors its emojis, alternates, ✅ when it mirrors back. 3 sessions/day.
- **chaotic**: random emojis from the keyboard, random signs, no language. 3 sessions/day.
- **minimalist**: short sessions twice a day, passes 💬 a lot, few emojis. 2 sessions/day.

## Goals → measurements

| goal | measured as | target |

## Comparison with v0.4 (before the v6 changes)

The v0.4 baseline with its interpretation: [broad-test-v0.4-baseline.md](broad-test-v0.4-baseline.md).

| goal | v0.4 | v0.5 (this run) | target | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| new stage every ~3 sessions | teacher 3 / 4 / 5 / rarely; affectionate & chaotic never | **4 / 7 / 9 / 12** for all engaged players; minimalist slower | ~3 / 6 / 9 / 12 | ✓ |
| session length, fully developed | 9.8 min (116 inputs) | **6.6 min** (80 inputs) | 5–10 | ✓ |
| not repetitive (teacher / devoted) | 14–31% | **12–16%** | < 15% | ~✓ |
| surprises from the creature (teacher) | 23–30% initiative, 1–2 new emojis | **36–42%**, 2–4 new emojis, memories, more games | steady | ✓ |
| adapts (shifter after switch) | 0–27%, never recovers | **25–35%** right away, old answers fade to ~0 | adapts | ✓ |
| inborn character | fixed forever | still all 6 after a week, then gradually replaced (teacher 2.5 of 6 by day 10); slower with players who reinforce little | persists, then changes | ✓ |
| early experiences | 80–89% of its moves on day 10 | 56–70% (shifter 21%) | fading | ~✓ |
| emotional range | only joyful / curious | joyful, curious, uneasy, sad (angry still never dominant) | wider | ~ |
| moulded by a teacher | 45–50% first-try | **30–38%** first-try, 3–5 active habits | high | trade-off |

**Trade-off:** more surprise and less looping cost some strict "obedience". About a third of its
direct replies follow your language, and the rest is variation, twists, memories and new ideas. The
test-panel sliders shift the balance: lower *chaos* / *novelty*, or fewer *reps to learn*, make it
more obedient.
